Fundaigo does not guarantee funding, determine final eligibility, provide legal advice, or submit applications automatically. Every result is a starting point for your team's own judgment, not a decision made on your behalf.

One Groq API key, a browser, and 30 minutes a week is all it takes. Any small nonprofit in education can clone this, update the org profile, and run it immediately.

In plain terms, this stage searches current funding sources, validates source links, compares eligibility against your org profile, flags risk or questionable output, organizes approved leads, and supports evidence-grounded proposal drafting. The table below is the technical detail behind that: which model runs each step, and its usage limit.

    Team data: org-owned Shared Drive
    
        
    
          Grant data lives in a Google Sheet inside your org's Shared Drive,
          not on any third-party server, and not owned by any one person's account. If a
          volunteer who's a team member leaves, removing them from the
          Shared Drive's membership is all that's needed; the data stays.
        
    
        
    

        
    

        
    
          
    
            
            
          
    
          
          
        
    

        
    
          One-time setup (founder/admin does this once):
          1) In Google Drive, click New → Shared Drive, name it (e.g.
          "Fundaigo Grant Data"), and add your grant team as members.
          2) Open it. The URL looks like
          drive.google.com/drive/folders/<ID>
          , copy the <ID> part.
          3) Paste it above and click Save. The team spreadsheet is created
          inside it automatically the first time anyone loads data.
